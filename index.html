BepsBot is a dual-mode LLM writing assistant for bipolar-disorder peer-support communities. Research Statement

Building Trustworthy, Grounded, and Auditable AI Systems

1. Research Vision

My research investigates how multi-agent architectures and large language models can support decision-making in high-stakes human contexts—ranging from industrial petrochemical supply chains to mental health peer-support communities. As LLMs transition from open-ended chat assistants to critical enterprise nodes, probabilistic generation introduces unacceptable variance and hallucination risks. I design and evaluate system architectures that combine foundation models with deterministic governance, dynamic retrieval, and human-in-the-loop validation to ensure AI outputs remain capable, auditable, and aligned with human values.

2. Methodological Framework

My research framework integrates three complementary methodologies:

  • Governance-First System Design: Locking business-critical numbers and evidence prior to LLM narration via finite-state machine (FSM) pre-rendering, enforced by deterministic validation guardrails.
  • Hybrid Context Grounding: Fusing structured enterprise truth (ERP databases, SQL views, time-series forecasts) with unstructured evidence through semantic RAG (ChromaDB, vector embeddings, entity linking).
  • Empirical System Validation: Evaluating reliability through walk-forward MAPE backtesting against locked baselines, automated LLM-as-a-Judge audit gates, and domain-expert annotation studies.

3. Future Directions

My ongoing and prospective research focuses on three core frontiers:

  • Causal Multi-Agent Reasoning: Extending state-machine orchestrators to perform counterfactual reasoning, temporal walk-forward backtesting, and self-correcting plan execution.
  • Multimodal & Edge Context Grounding: Integrating embedded sensor streams (ESP32, IoT hardware) with lightweight edge models for real-time visual and environmental context grounding.
  • Human-AI Co-Decision Dynamics: Quantifying trust, cognitive load, and safety alignment when domain experts collaborate with autonomous multi-agent systems in safety-critical workflows.
